I'm Sam, a postdoctoral fellow at Caltech with David Van Valen in the Biology and Biological Engineering division. My current work follows a few common threads: optical pooled screening, deep learning tools for microscopy image analysis, and biosensor design and engineering. The unifying theme of my research is to develop tools that take advantage of the beautiful diversity that biomolecules have to offer. For more information, check out my [research](/research/)!

When I'm not in the lab, I am a passionate practitioner of ceramics, fiber arts, and music.
